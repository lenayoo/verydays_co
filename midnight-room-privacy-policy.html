Midnight Room Privacy Policy

Midnight Room is designed to help you slow down at night without accounts, ads, or behavioral tracking.

No sign-in On-device preferences No ads or tracking

Summary

Midnight Room is built to provide a quiet, low-friction experience. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not use advertising SDKs or behavioral tracking tools inside the app.

Information We Collect

Midnight Room does not collect personal information or device-based identifiers through normal use of the app. If the app remembers your selected room, playback preferences, or similar settings, that information is intended to remain on your device.

How We Use Information

Any settings stored by the app are used only to maintain your experience inside Midnight Room. We do not use your information for profiling, advertising, selling data, or behavioral analytics.

Support Requests

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and any information you include in your message. We use that information only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, and keep a basic record of support conversations.

Third-Party Services and Store Providers

Midnight Room does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics trackers. If you download the app through the Apple App Store or Google Play, those platforms may process transaction, device, or account information under their own privacy policies.

Permissions, Backups, and Local Data

Midnight Room is designed to deliver its core experience without requiring access to your contacts, photos, or precise location. If your device platform includes app data in system backups or crash reports, that handling is controlled by Apple or Google according to your settings and their policies.

Children's Privacy

Because Midnight Room does not collect personal information through normal app use, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Your Choices

Since normal app use does not send personal data to our servers, there is generally no server-side retention of your in-app activity. If you email support, we keep that correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain support records. You may ask us to delete past support emails by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or want to request deletion of a support email you sent, contact us at lenayoo17@gmail.com.
